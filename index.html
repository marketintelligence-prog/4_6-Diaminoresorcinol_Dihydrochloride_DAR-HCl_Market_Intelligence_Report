Executive Summary

What this product is and why it matters

DAR·2HCl is the bis-o-aminophenol monomer that is polycondensed with terephthalic acid in polyphosphoric acid to make PBO, the highest-strength commercial organic fibre (≈5.8 GPa tensile, 180–280 GPa modulus, LOI 68) [35]. Monomer purity governs polymer molecular weight, and DAR is a strong reducing agent that oxidises readily, making synthesis, purification, storage and transport demanding [34]. It has no meaningful use outside benzobisoxazole polymers and research.

Supply is extremely concentrated. Toyobo (Japan) built the first commercial PBO plant in 1998 under the Zylon brand [34]; China built a domestic chain after years of import dependence and export restrictions on DAR and PBO [45][35]. Zhejiang Dinglong Technology holds 200 t of approved PBO-monomer capacity and supplies domestic fibre makers (Chengdu Xinchen, Zhongke Jinqi, Zhonglan Chenguang) — "only the domestic market" [26].

New demand vectors are real but early: PBO backplates for foldable phones (Sinochem: 0.22 mm vs 0.4–0.6 mm glass) [37] and PBO tendons for humanoid-robot dexterous hands, where cost is currently the barrier [34].

India lens: India has no PBO fibre producer and no identified commercial DAR consumer; demand is gram-to-kg research use via catalogue houses [11]. India does, however, have the two key route feedstocks domestically — 1,2,3-trichlorobenzene (Aarti Industries) [50] and resorcinol (Atul, sole producer) [51] — giving a credible "China+1" manufacturing story if an export anchor customer is secured.

Synonyms: DAR·HCl, DAR·2HCl, 4,6-diamino-1,3-benzenediol dihydrochloride, 4,6-dihydroxy-1,3-phenylenediamine dihydrochloride, 4,6-diaminoresorcinol 2HCl, 1,3-benzenediol, 4,6-diamino-, hydrochloride (1:2) [9][5]. Chinese: 4,6-二氨基间苯二酚盐酸盐.

DAR carries two ortho-aminophenol pairs on one benzene ring. Each pair cyclises with a carboxylic acid to form an oxazole ring, so one DAR plus one terephthalic acid unit builds the fused, rod-like benzobisoxazole backbone of PBO. Sigma-Aldrich lists it as a precursor to PBO and to naphthalene-based benzobisoxazole polymers (Naph-2,6-PBO, Naph-1,5-PBO) [7][14]. The free base oxidises rapidly in air, so commerce is almost entirely in the dihydrochloride salt, and stabilised compositions containing a reducing agent are patented [25].

India strategic note: India has the whole raw-material chain for route 1: 1,2,3-TCB from Aarti [50], nitration/hydrogenation skills across Gujarat and Maharashtra, and captive HCl. The core route patents date from 1986–1997 (e.g., US 4,766,244 issued 1988) [15] and have long expired. A cGMP-style pilot at 1–5 t/yr in an existing multipurpose nitration/hydrogenation block is the low-capex entry.

Feasibility Summary & Management Recommendation

Overall verdict
CONDITIONAL

DAR·2HCl is a strategically important but structurally small (≈400–900 t/yr), captive and China-centric market. India has zero commercial demand. Margins exist at the gram-to-kilogram tier (US$1,000–13,000/kg) and potentially in non-China export of polymer-grade material (≈US$42–44/kg bulk benchmark).

We recommend entering through CRO/custom synthesis and catalogue export, supported by a qualified Chinese source for trading. Manufacturing stays gated until a non-China anchor customer commits to at least 50 t/yr.
